# StaTech planning data

The default catalog contains planning facts extracted from the released StaTech Industry 2 version 2.0.1: resource identities, recipe amounts, resolved ingredient choices, machine behavior, structures, and progression references. It does not contain Minecraft or mod executables, textures, sounds, quest prose, original recipe scripts, or world saves.

StaTech Industry is maintained by TheStaticVoid and contributors. The reference release is https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/statech-industry-2/files/8875911. Its source is https://github.com/TheStaticVoid/StaTech-Industry at commit 9b7ef18b5dfdcb3ac8eda93cb6dd2ca6135cbaf9, with a GNU GPL version 3 license. The matching license text is included in data/licenses/statech.txt.

Machine evidence comes from Modern Industrialization by Azercoco, Technici4n, and contributors; Extended Industrialization and Tesseract by Swedz and contributors; Industrialization Overdrive by Phantom-n-Co and contributors; Yet Another Industrialization by Luligabi and contributors; and the other mods recorded in the distribution audit. AE2 and ME Requester sources establish the tested save formats. Matching source commits are recorded in data/provenance/sources.json, and the primary source license notices are in data/licenses/.

The distribution audit records each inspected mod archive, its hash, declared license, authors, and license-file hashes. Recipe namespaces can differ from mod IDs: culturalrecipes belongs to Cultural Delights, and modtemplate recipes come from Craft Config. A namespace alone does not prove who supplied a recipe; pack scripts can replace recipes from other namespaces. The released pack and pinned extraction inputs remain the reference.

Upstream names and license declarations identify the source projects. They do not grant permission to redistribute the games, mods, or assets. This planner distributes its normalized observations and calculation rules, with explicit reports for unsupported behavior. The bundled catalog is still incomplete; see the project issues and the catalog's completeness flag.
